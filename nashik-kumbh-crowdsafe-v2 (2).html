From concept to GIS

How CrowdSafe could work

A transparent path from a visual prototype to a more evidence-based disaster-mitigation tool.

01

Map the real locations

Use verified GIS layers for ghats, roads, gates, public facilities and temporary structures. Confirm data source, date and coordinate accuracy.

02

Assess crowd pressure

Combine approved crowd observations and site-capacity assumptions to flag potential bottlenecks. Do not label zones as live risk without live data.

03

Plan & validate response

Display only routes and aid points checked by authorized local teams. Review access, closures, accessibility and evacuation capacity before real use.
